Cookie Policy & Digital Preferences

1. Principles of Digital Restraint and Student Privacy

LessonLadder operates under an uncompromising commitment to digital transparency, distraction-free learning ergonomics, and ethical data privacy. We believe your study sessions and academic research should remain private and unencumbered by invasive third-party ad trackers. This Cookie Policy explains transparently how essential cookies and local storage function across our adaptive learning platform.

2. Strictly Necessary Technical & Authentication Cookies

Essential technical cookies are required for the secure operation, cryptographic verification, and rendering stability of our learning console. These cookies maintain encrypted SSL/TLS session states, verify student login authentication tokens across course modules, prevent cross-site request forgery attacks, and ensure stable progress telemetry saving during interactive assessments. Essential cookies cannot be disabled without breaking core platform security.

3. Local Storage for Theme Lighting and Typography Scaling

To protect student visual comfort during extended study sessions, LessonLadder utilizes client-side HTML5 Local Storage to remember your chosen interface theme (Academic Indigo, Cyber Blue Console, or Daylight Clean Campus) and your in-article typography scaling preferences. This configuration data resides strictly within your local device browser sandbox and is never transmitted to commercial marketing servers.

4. Anonymized Measurement & Performance Telemetry

We employ privacy-compliant Google Analytics 4 telemetry (Google Tag `G-0LY0HY7L01`) to evaluate aggregate platform loading latencies, feature engagement rates, and device display dimensions. All visitor IP addresses are automatically pseudonymized at the point of ingestion, ensuring that no individual student identity or institutional record is ever linked to browsing patterns.

5. Browser Management and Cookie Controls

Users maintain full control to review, restrict, or clear stored cookies and local storage entries at any time through web browser preferences (supported in Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and Edge). Please note that clearing essential storage may require you to re-authenticate your institutional session.

6. Governance Inquiries and Data Protection Contact

For questions regarding our cookie policies or to discuss institutional data governance standards, please contact our data stewardship desk at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or contact us by telephone at +1-888-777-5845.
