Student Privacy Policy & Educational Data Protection

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • FERPA & COPPA Certified Standards

1. Commitment to Student Confidentiality and Educational Data Ethics

At LessonLadder, we hold the privacy, digital security, and academic confidentiality of students, educators, and partner institutions in the highest regard. In an era where learning telemetry is frequently exploited for commercial profiling, LessonLadder is built upon strict data minimization principles, full compliance with the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA), the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), and global data protection frameworks.

2. Categories of Academic Data Collected Across Our Learning Console

To deliver adaptive skill scaffolding and calculate personalized spaced repetition schedules, we collect educational performance metrics including task completion latency, diagnostic answer choices, rubric scores, and user account parameters (name, institutional email address, and grade level). Technical telemetry including IP addresses and device browser viewports is collected solely to maintain secure server connections and prevent unauthorized account access.

3. Purpose and Lawful Basis for Pedagogical Data Processing

Student data is processed exclusively to deliver individualized learning ladders, calculate Ebbinghaus retention curves, provide formative analytics to verified teachers, and improve the mathematical precision of our cognitive algorithms. Anonymized, de-identified research aggregates may be analyzed by our cognitive science faculty to advance evidence-based pedagogy under strict institutional review board (IRB) ethical standards.

4. Absolute Prohibition on Commercial Data Monetization & Third-Party Sharing

LessonLadder enforces an absolute, legally binding commitment: we never sell, rent, monetize, or disclose student records, behavioral telemetry, or academic performance profiles to third-party advertisers, data brokers, or commercial marketing entities. We share necessary operational data strictly with SOC 2 certified cloud infrastructure providers and institutional school administrators possessing legitimate educational interests.

5. Cryptographic Data Security and Institutional Retention Schedules

All student interactions, diagnostic assessments, and gradebook records are protected in transit using TLS 1.3 encryption and at rest using AES-256 military-grade encryption. Academic records are retained only for the duration of an institution active licensing agreement or as required by statutory educational recordkeeping laws. Upon account termination or graduation, all personal student identifiers are permanently purged or irreversibly anonymized.

6. Universal Student Rights and Data Protection Officer Contact

Under FERPA, GDPR, and state student privacy legislation, parents and adult students maintain complete legal rights to inspect, review, correct, or request the deletion of their educational records. To exercise these rights or discuss our institutional privacy architecture, please contact our Data Protection Officer at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or telephone our compliance desk at +1-888-777-5845.
